Accessibility statement

Pinnacle EDS is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with visible focus indicators.
  • Pages use headings and landmarks so screen reader users can navigate easily.
  • Text and controls meet minimum color contrast ratios, and text can be enlarged without losing content.
  • Images have text alternatives, and forms have clear labels and messages.
  • The site is offered in several languages, with a right-to-left layout for Arabic.
  • Animations can be paused, and they stop automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.

Known limitations

Some translations are drafts that are still being reviewed by native speakers.

Feedback and assistance

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, email edspinnacle@gmail.com. Please describe the page and the problem. We aim to respond within five business days.
