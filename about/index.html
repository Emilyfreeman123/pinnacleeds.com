Our story

One firm for work its founders were already doing.

After years of being pulled toward this work while serving in other roles, our founders came together with a single mandate: build the firm that could deliver what they had each been doing for decades, with the scale and structure institutions need.

Large organizations and governments set the conditions for whether children receive quality special education. When those systems are well built, children thrive. When they are fragmented or missing, children fall through the gaps. Our work exists to close those gaps at the institutional level, where the most children can be reached.

Every child, wherever they live or go to school, deserves a system built to serve them.

Our mission

Systems that serve children, families, and communities.

For children

Consistent, evidence-based ABA and special education services, whichever school, clinic, or country a child is in.

For families

Transparency, consistency, and confidence in the care their children receive, with the support they need alongside it.

For countries

Governance that keeps quality consistent across every school in a nation, so geography never decides whether a child is served.

Emily Freeman, BCBA

Managing Director, Clinical Systems

Emily leads Pinnacle EDS's clinical systems practice. Her work centers on the infrastructure that keeps applied behavior analysis consistent across clinicians and locations: treatment plan standards, session delivery, supervision, and documentation.

She guides the firm's clinical and compliance frameworks for health systems and multi-site ABA providers, from the first organizational assessment to quality assurance across every site.
