How we work

From assessment to independence, in five phases.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined path and ends with infrastructure your teams can run without us.

  1. Assessment

    Audit of current systems, compliance posture, and gaps, with a prioritized roadmap.

  2. System design

    Frameworks, clinical standards, and policy documents built for your scale.

  3. Implementation

    Hands-on rollout across your organization or ministry.

  4. Training

    Workforce development so your teams operate the system independently.

  5. Ongoing support

    Advisory, compliance monitoring, and system evolution over time.

All engagements are governed by our Institutional Implementation Methodology (IIM), which defines phased delivery, governance, risk management, and long-term sustainability.

Frameworks

Named systems, ready to deploy.

Each framework is a defined engagement built from our team's clinical and educational leadership experience.

Clinical and complianceFor health systems and multi-site ABA providers.

  • CIA
    Clinical Infrastructure AssessmentAn organizational audit of program design, documentation, scheduling, supervision, and compliance.
  • ISF
    ITP Standardization FrameworkConsistent treatment plan development, goal architecture, and program design across every clinician and location.
  • SDS
    7.5-Minute Service Delivery SystemA scheduling and delivery method that targets, tracks, and executes clinical goals within every session.
  • MQAS
    Multi-Site Quality Assurance SystemStandardized documentation, supervisory review, and clinical auditing across locations and jurisdictions.

Education and workforceFor state agencies, districts, and training pipelines.

  • SESRA
    Special Education Systems Readiness AssessmentEvaluation of infrastructure, workforce capacity, and compliance alignment ahead of systemic reform.
  • NWDF
    National Workforce Development FrameworkTraining pipelines from foundational educator competencies to advanced practitioner and train-the-trainer programs.

GovernmentFor ministries and national agencies.

  • GRPA
    Government Readiness & Policy AssessmentReview of policy, infrastructure gaps, and workforce readiness, with a pathway to national implementation.
