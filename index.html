How we work

From assessment to independence, in five phases.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined path and ends with infrastructure your teams can run without us.

  1. Assessment

    Audit of current systems, compliance posture, and gaps, with a prioritized roadmap.

  2. System design

    Frameworks, clinical standards, and policy documents built for your scale.

  3. Implementation

    Hands-on rollout across your organization or ministry.

  4. Training

    Workforce development so your teams operate the system independently.

  5. Ongoing support

    Advisory, compliance monitoring, and system evolution over time.

All engagements are governed by our Institutional Implementation Methodology (IIM), which defines phased delivery, governance, risk management, and long-term sustainability.

FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Short answers to what organizations and ministries ask us first. Have another question? Contact us.

What does Pinnacle EDS do?

We design and implement the clinical, educational, and policy systems that institutions use to deliver special education and ABA services consistently. That includes assessments, frameworks, quality and documentation systems, and the training your teams need to run them.

Who do you work with?

Health systems and multi-site ABA providers, state education agencies and large school districts, and governments and ministries of education.

How does an engagement work?

Every engagement moves through five phases: assessment, system design, implementation, training, and ongoing support. It is governed by our Institutional Implementation Methodology and designed so your teams can run the system without us.

Do you work outside the United States?

Yes. We work virtually and on site, including with governments in the Gulf region and the wider Middle East and North Africa. When local legal or regulatory interpretation is needed, we coordinate with qualified counsel in that jurisdiction.

Do you provide therapy or services directly to children?

No. We provide consulting and systems design for organizations. Clinical and educational recommendations are carried out by your own appropriately licensed professionals.

Where is your team licensed?

Our practitioners hold Licensed Behavior Analyst credentials in Minnesota, Virginia, Kansas, Maryland, and Hawaii, and teaching licensure in California, Minnesota, and Hawaii.

How do we get started?

Send us a message or email info@pinnacleeds.com, and we will reply to schedule a discovery call. Ministries, please include your country and ministry.
